Hitherto the Lord Hath Helped Us

Sample note25 October 2026 · KCC Teaching Team · Series: 34 Years of Grace · 1 Samuel 7:7–14

“Then Samuel took a stone… and called the name of it Ebenezer, saying, Hitherto hath the Lord helped us.”1 Samuel 7:12 (KJV)

The Big Idea

Remembering God's faithfulness in the past gives us confidence to trust Him with the future.

01Raise your Ebenezer

Samuel set up a stone so Israel would never forget. Since 1992, KCC has its own stones of remembrance — prayers answered, lives changed, families restored.

  • Gratitude is a spiritual discipline, not just a feeling.
  • Tell your testimony — it builds someone else's faith.
  • Write down what God has done for you this year.

02Help came after repentance

Before victory, Israel put away their idols and returned to the Lord. Grace finds a people who return to Him with all their hearts.

  • Revival begins with surrender, not strategy.
  • What “idols” compete for first place in our lives?

03“Hitherto” means the story continues

Hitherto — up to this point. Thirty-four years are a testimony, not a finish line. The same God carries us forward.

  • Past faithfulness is a down payment on future faithfulness.
  • Every generation must encounter God for itself.
Key ThoughtThe God who helped us this far will not leave us now.

For your cell group

  • What is one “Ebenezer” moment in your life?
  • How can we pass on KCC's story to new members and the next generation?

Prayer

Lord, thank You for 34 years of grace at KCC. As You have helped us, so lead us into the years ahead. Amen.
