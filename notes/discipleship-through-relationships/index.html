Discipleship Through Relationships

Sample note18 October 2026 · KCC Teaching Team · Series: Foundations · Acts 2:42–47

“And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers.”Acts 2:42 (KJV)

The Big Idea

We grow best in the company of others — Christ makes disciples through people who walk with people.

01The early church was devoted

Four commitments marked the first believers: the Word, fellowship, the breaking of bread and prayer. They are still our blueprint.

  • Teaching shapes what we believe.
  • Fellowship shapes who we become.
  • Prayer keeps us dependent on God.

02Relationships are the delivery system

Jesus chose twelve “that they should be with him.” Discipleship is caught as much as it is taught.

  • Everyone needs a Paul, a Barnabas and a Timothy.
  • Cell groups are where relationships become real.
  • Pastoring is personal at KCC.

03Healthy community grows

“The Lord added to the church daily.” Growth was the fruit of a loving, generous, praying community.

  • Open homes open hearts.
  • Generosity is a witness to our neighbours.
Key ThoughtYou cannot be a fully devoted follower of Christ alone.

For your cell group

  • Who is discipling you right now? Who are you discipling?
  • What would make our cell group more like Acts 2?

Prayer

Jesus, connect me with the people You will use to grow me, and make me a blessing in someone else's walk. Amen.
