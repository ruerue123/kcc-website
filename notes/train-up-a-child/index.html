Raising Champions: Train Up a Child

Sample note1 November 2026 · KCC Teaching Team · Series: Family · Proverbs 22:6

“Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.”Proverbs 22:6 (KJV)

The Big Idea

Children are not the church of tomorrow — they are disciples today, and the whole church shares in raising them.

01Training is intentional

“Train” speaks of dedication and direction. Godly children are not an accident; they are the fruit of consistent investment.

  • Model faith at home — children learn more by sight than by sound.
  • Make the Word part of everyday life (Deuteronomy 6:6–7).
  • Celebrate small wins and godly character.

02Meet children where they are

Jesus took children in His arms. Through Raising Champions we meet children on the football field and introduce them to the One who loves them.

  • Play opens doors that preaching alone cannot.
  • Every child on the field is a soul we are responsible for.
  • Volunteers: coaching, first aid, refreshments and prayer.

03It takes a church

Discipleship through relationships includes the next generation. Every adult can be a spiritual parent, uncle or aunt.

  • Know the names of the children in your row.
  • Pray for a child by name this week.
  • Invite a family to Saturday football.
Key ThoughtWhat we sow into children today, the nation will harvest tomorrow.

For your cell group

  • Who poured faith into you as a child?
  • How can you practically invest in a child at KCC this month?
  • What habits in our homes teach children about God?

Prayer

Father, raise champions for Christ in Kuwadzana. Make our homes and our church places where children meet You. Amen.
