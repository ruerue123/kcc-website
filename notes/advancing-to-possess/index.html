Advancing to Possess

Sample note8 November 2026 · KCC Teaching Team · Series: Year of Jubilee · Joshua 1:1–9

“Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon, that have I given unto you.”Joshua 1:3 (KJV)

The Big Idea

God has already given the promise; our part is to rise, step forward in faith and take possession.

01The promise comes before the possession

Before Israel crossed the Jordan, God spoke in the past tense: “I have given you.” Jubilee is a season of restoration, but restored things must still be walked into.

  • God's promises are settled in heaven before they are seen on earth.
  • Delay is not denial — Moses died, but the promise did not.
  • Ask: what has God already said over my life, family and work?

02Possession requires movement

“Arise, go over this Jordan.” The land did not come to Joshua; Joshua had to go to the land.

  • Faith has feet: prayer must be followed by obedience.
  • Your next step is usually small and specific.
  • Comfort zones are the enemies of inheritance.

03Courage is commanded, not felt

Three times God tells Joshua to be strong and courageous. Courage is not the absence of fear but obedience in its presence.

  • Courage grows from meditating on the Word day and night.
  • God's presence is the source: “I will be with thee.”
  • Courage is contagious — leaders set the tone for families.
Key ThoughtJubilee restores what was lost; advancing possesses what was promised.

For your cell group

  • Which promise of God are you still waiting on?
  • What is one concrete step of obedience you can take this week?
  • How can our cell group encourage you to be courageous?

Prayer

Lord, thank You for every promise You have spoken. Give me courage to arise and walk into all You have prepared. Amen.
