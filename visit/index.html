Good To Know

Your Questions

What time should I arrive?

The Sunday service starts at 08:30 AM. Come about 15 minutes early and our ushers will welcome you and help you find a seat.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. You'll see everything from Sunday best to casual. What matters is that you're here.

What about my children?

Children are welcome on Sundays, and every Saturday kids can join Raising Champions football on our grounds.

Where is the church?

We meet at 104168 1st Street, Kuwadzana 6, Harare. Tap the map on this page for directions.

How do I get involved?

Start with a cell group near you. From there you can serve in one of our ministries. Just tell us what you love doing.
