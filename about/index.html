About KCC

Who We Are

A Faith Ministries family in Kuwadzana

Kuwadzana Community Church (KCC) is the Faith Ministries church in Kuwadzana 6, Harare. Since 1992 we have been a home for worship, the Word and genuine relationships, one family with our sister churches across Zimbabwe.

Faith Ministries celebrates 50 years since its founding in 1976: a Jubilee for every assembly, KCC included.

Principles Of Ministry

Our Principles

The Foundation Of Ministry Is Character

Character is more important than ministry. Ministry is a gift from God whilst character is something you are responsible for.

Matthew 7:22-23

The Nature Of Ministry Is Service

Jesus came not to be served but to serve. Greatness in the house of God is measured by service.

Matthew 20:26-28

The Motive Of Ministry Is Love

Everything God does flows from love, so everything we do must too: love the Lord, love the brethren, love the lost.

1 Corinthians 13

The Authority Of Ministry Is Submission

Those who obey can be trusted with authority.

Hebrews 13:17

The Measure Of Ministry Is Sacrifice

God has given to every person a measure of faith; we give ourselves fully in return.

Romans 12:1-3

Statement Of Faith

What We Believe

The Scriptures

The Old and New Testaments are inspired by God — the infallible, authoritative rule of faith and conduct.

One God

One true and living God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

Fully God and fully man: born of a virgin, sinless, crucified for our sins, risen bodily, ascended and interceding for us.

Salvation

By grace through faith in Christ — a new birth by the Holy Spirit into the family of God.

The Holy Spirit

Present and active today: convicting, regenerating, sanctifying and empowering believers for ministry.

Divine healing

Deliverance from sickness and affliction is provided for in the atonement.
